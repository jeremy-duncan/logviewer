      Search syntax
      failed password
      Terms are combined with AND and matched anywhere in the line. Quote phrases.
      program:sshd
      Field contains the text, ignoring case.
      src=10.1.*
      Field equals the value. * and ? are wildcards. host=* matches lines that have the field.
      message~"failed .* from"
      Field matches a regular expression.
      -program:cron
      A leading minus excludes matches.
      severity=err OR severity=crit
      OR separates groups of ANDed terms.
      src="fe80::1"
      IPv6 addresses work as plain terms or as quoted field values. A bare token such as host:port is read as a field, so quote it to search for the text.
